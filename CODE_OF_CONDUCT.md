# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be civil, stay on the technical question, and assume the other person is acting
in good faith. That is the whole expectation.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion on the technical substance: what was observed, what was
  expected, and what evidence supports the claim.
- Disagree with the argument rather than the person.
- Accept that maintainers will decline changes. A declined change is a scope
  decision, not a judgement of the contributor.
- Respect the reporting boundaries in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md): report
  vulnerabilities privately, and never post credentials, customer data, or
  production identifiers in a public issue or pull request.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or a group.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled decisions without new evidence,
  or pressing a request after it has been answered.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
other space where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report a concern through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this
repository ("Security" → "Report a vulnerability"), which reaches the
maintainers privately. Describe what happened and link to the relevant thread.

Reports are read by the maintainers. A reporter will not be named publicly
without their agreement.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may block an account, when this
document is not followed. The response is proportionate to the conduct, and
repeated or deliberate breaches lead to a permanent block.

Maintainers are held to this document on the same terms as everyone else.
